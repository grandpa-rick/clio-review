## UID 390 — delta is quadratic

**Claim.** K is finite of class <= 2, M = Inn K is abelian, and the base is T^2. A band rho is a commuting pair (Abar, Bbar) in Out K, and delta(rho) = [A,B] in M_{(sigma,tau)}.
- Thm 1 (master formula, on the nose in M): [A1A2,B1B2] = [A1,B1] + s1t1[A2,B2] + s1[A2,B1] + t1[A1,B2].
- Thm 5: on Out^0 = ker(Out -> Aut M), delta(r1r2) = delta1 + delta2 + chi with chi = c(A1,B2) + c(A2,B1), and delta(r^n) = n^2 delta(r).
- Thm 6: for abelian Abar ⊆ Out^0, (1) additive <=> (2) delta==0 <=> (3) c|Abar==0 <=> (4) pi^-1(Abar) abelian.
- Headline: "no abelian subgroup on which delta is a nonzero homomorphism; a coupled torus is always a witness to non-additivity".

**Proof check.**
- The two commutator identities are correct for [x,y] = xyx^-1y^-1; I expanded both by hand.
- Thm 1: the expansion and the prefixes A1, A1B1, 1, B1 are correct.
- Lemma 4: bi-multiplicativity uses the centrality of M in Aut^0 K, which is true by the definition of Out^0.
- Thm 5 follows from Lemma 4.
- Thm 6: (1)=>(3) via the bands (x,1) and (1,y) is correct. (3)<=>(4) is the standard fact about a central extension of an abelian group.
- All correct.
